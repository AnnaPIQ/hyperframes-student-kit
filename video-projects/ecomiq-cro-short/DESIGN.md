# ecomiq-cro-short — Design Spec (9:16)

Format: 9:16 Story/Reels · 1080x1920 @ 30fps · Safe area ~10% margins (96px sides).
Sibling 4:5 export: `../ecomiq-cro-short-45/` — generated from this project's
`index.html` by `scripts/make-45.py`, never edited by hand.

Brand kit copied from `assets/ecomiq/`. Full reference: `assets/ecomiq/BRAND.md`.
Tokens in `assets/brand-tokens.css`; Rethink Sans + Hedvig Letters Serif as
local `.woff2` in `assets/fonts/`. GSAP vendored at `assets/vendor/gsap.min.js`.

Full edit rationale, shot map and source probes: **`EDIT_PLAN.md`**.

## This project's idea
- **Hook:** "Want more of your Shopify traffic to actually buy?"
- **Message:** you already paid for the traffic; 90 days with an EcomIQ
  strategist turns more of it into sales.
- **CTA:** EcomIQ end card + "Link Below", cutting in at 16.87s on the line
  "more sales from the traffic you've already got" and holding 6.03s.

## Structure
Sean's VO is the spine and the only audio. The Showcase Reel montage plays
underneath it, muted and reordered so each shot block lands on the beat it
sells. Sean never appears as a talking head — montage throughout, his voice
over the top.

## Type
- Rethink Sans 800 for every display mark, −2%…−4% tracking on the big ones.
- **Exactly one** Hedvig Letters Serif italic in the piece: *days*, under the
  90 counter. Brand rule — never a second emphasis word.

## Colour
Flame `#FF4C32` is the only hot accent (GUARANTEED pill, BUYERS bar, the
VISITORS→SALES arrow and tag, the end-card button). Blue tint `#9CD4FF` carries
the cool side. Navy `#06284C` is the canvas and every scrim.

## What NOT to do
- No captions. The graphics are sparse emphasis marks, not a word track.
- No percentage or stat labels on the conversion-gap bars — the script makes no
  numeric claim, so the graphic must not either.
- Don't crop the 4:5 out of the 9:16 master; it drops 29.7% of the height and
  clips heads. It comes off the square master. See `EDIT_PLAN.md`.
- Don't hand-edit `../ecomiq-cro-short-45/index.html`.
