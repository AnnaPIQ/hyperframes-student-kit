## This project's idea
Founder testimonial (real A-roll from DOTDEV 2026) intercut with cinematic
villa/laptop b-roll and EcomIQ brand motion. ~35.3s, 9:16.

- **Hook:** "People ask us why we fly around the world for these big Shopify and
  e-commerce events…" (founder, full-bleed, on-mic at the conference)
- **Message:** the e-commerce industry moves so fast that last year's advice goes
  stale — EcomIQ gives you *what's working right now, tested and explained*, not
  last year's playbook.
- **CTA:** navy outro — "Know what's *working* right now." + flame "Get started →".

See `STORYBOARD.md` for the full beat sheet, audio splice, and cutaway timing.
Prepped assets: `aroll-cut.mp4` (spliced founder VO+video), `broll-establish.mp4`,
`broll-work.mp4`. Final output renders to `renders/ecomiq-short-form-ad.mp4`
(gitignored). Audio is founder VO only — drop a licensed music bed into `assets/`
and add a ducked sibling `<audio>` if desired.
