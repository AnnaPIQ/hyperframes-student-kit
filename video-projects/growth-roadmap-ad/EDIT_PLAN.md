# Growth Roadmap Ad, Editing Plan (9:16 Meta)

**Build:** `video-projects/growth-roadmap-ad/` · 1080x1920 · 30fps · 40.6s · VO normalised to -16 LUFS · **sound: Sean's voice only, no music, no SFX**
**Ad timecode = A-roll source timecode** (the A-roll runs untrimmed underneath the whole ad).
All timings below come from a word-level transcription of the supplied A-roll, not from the written script.

### What Sean actually says (subtitles follow the audio)
> Shopify founders doing $20,000 a month and aiming for $100,000. We'll show you what's holding your growth back and what to fix first. Your strategist goes through your numbers with you and finds where the ceiling is. Then they bring in specialists from the team that have helped brands like Mob Armor grow total sales by over 500% in a year. They guide your team through the changes and track the result. And it all starts with a free discovery call. We'll go through where you are, where you want to be, and what it would take to get you there. And if that gap isn't one that we think we can close, we'll tell you. So tap Book Now and choose a time for your free discovery call.

Differences from the written script: "helped **brands like** Mob Armor", "**we'll tell you**" (not "we'll say so"), "what it would take **to get you there**", "**And it all** starts", and the CTA adds "**for your free discovery call**".

### Fixed layout (every frame)
| Zone | Y range (of 1920) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sean's face | ~200 to 1030 | never covered while he is on screen |
| Roadmap / proof card | 1040 to 1290 | chest band, navy card |
| Subtitles | bottom edge locked at 1450, 1 to 2 lines grow upward | identical on Sean, B-roll and graphics |
| Platform UI | 1470 to 1920 | kept clear |

### Screen-time split
| Layer | Seconds | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Sean on camera | 21.9s | 54% |
| Full-screen roadmap graphics | 11.7s | 29% |
| B-roll | 7.0s | 17% |

B-roll sits below the 30 to 40% guide on purpose. Three of the brief's strongest analysis clips have no Drive link in the sheet (see Part 2 notes), and the remaining footage is used only where it proves something. It can be raised to ~25% by extending the strategist beat if you want more cover.

| | Profit / margin diagnostic ad | 3 Changes / 30 Minutes ad | **This ad** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core device | leaks, margin, diagnostic | 01 / 02 / 03 cards, clock / audit | **CURRENT ● → ○ TARGET road** |

**Distinct through:** current vs target, $20K → $100K, growth ceiling, blockers, the gap, roadmap, milestones (CHANGE MADE, RESULT TRACKED, BLOCKERS, PRIORITIES), progress path, YOU ARE HERE. No numbered cards, no timers, no leak or margin imagery.

**Brand-consistent through:** the exact palette (no extra hues, no green for positive, no gradients), Rethink Sans + Hedvig serif italic (one emphasis word per headline), the bottom subtitle system, the Flame pill CTA, navy card language, and calm premium motion.

**Revised during the build:** the blocker beat was kept as one ceiling that splits into cells, specifically to avoid reading as the 01/02/03 card system.
