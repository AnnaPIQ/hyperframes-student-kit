# BRIEF — PacificIQ × Duckhorn Six-Site Launch Sizzle

- **slug:** `duckhorn-sizzle`
- **intent:** Agency promotional sizzle reel — PacificIQ showcasing that it designed & launched all six Duckhorn Collection brand sites at once.
- **audience:** Prospective DTC / wine / premium e-commerce clients; PacificIQ's own marketing (website, LinkedIn, pitch decks, email).
- **dimensions:** 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **fps:** 30
- **duration:** ~30s (≈900 frames)
- **voice:** none (music bed only)
- **caption plan:** on-screen kinetic text + brand name labels only — no spoken-word captions
- **face-cam:** none

## Whose brand frames it
PacificIQ is the storyteller. The reel **opens and closes on the PacificIQ brand** (electric-blue field, monstera leaf mark, wordmark). The six Duckhorn sites are the *content* — the proof of work. The warm, editorial wine footage is the "color"; PacificIQ electric blue is the connective brand thread.

## Style profile
- **Primary brand color (PacificIQ):** `#0500ED` electric blue
- **Canvas / ink:** `#0A0A0C` near-black, `#141414` (PacificIQ dark)
- **Light tint:** `#E3E3FD` lavender-white (PacificIQ)
- **Accent (sparingly):** `#FAFF7E` lime (PacificIQ highlight) — used only for a single "live" / launch flourish
- **Wine warmth:** carried entirely by the footage (burgundy, gold, cream) — no extra warm hues introduced
- **Fonts:**
  - **Space Grotesk** (700/500) — PacificIQ voice (Aeonik substitute) for all overlay/headline text & wordmark
  - **JetBrains Mono** — small mono labels (URLs, "06 / 06" counters)
  - The wine sites carry their own editorial serifs *inside the footage* — we don't re-type them.
- **Logo:** official PacificIQ monstera leaf mark (`assets/piq-leaf.png`, pulled from pacificiq.com) + "PacificIQ" set in Space Grotesk. Swappable for an official vector if supplied.
- **Reference aesthetic:** MOTION_PHILOSOPHY *discipline* (one idea per beat, motion-blur whip transitions, dark canvas, grain + vignette on every scene, breathing outro, rule of threes/sixes) — but palette adapted to PacificIQ electric blue, not chrome.

## The six brands (content order)
1. **The Duckhorn Collection** — parent/portfolio. Burgundy, autumnal Napa estate. "Napa, at its most welcoming."
2. **Duckhorn Vineyards** — flagship. Cream, refined. "Napa Valley Estate Vineyards."
3. **Decoy** — modern, social, accessible. "Pour to what's possible."
4. **Goldeneye** — Anderson Valley Pinot. Moody, dark. "Crafted in small lots."
5. **Calera** — terroir/mountain, golden-hour. "A singular mountain. A radical belief."
6. **Greenwing** — clean, bottle-forward. "Rooted in remarkable places."

## Footage treatment
Each site shown as a **scroll-capture inside a rounded browser-frame card** (with a URL bar showing the domain) floating on the dark/electric-blue canvas. The recorded scroll motion sells "real, live website." Whip-pan transitions between brands. Pre-trimmed to clean ~3.6s 30fps segments (`assets/site-*.mp4`).

## Pacing
Kinetic. Open ~6s → six brands ~3.1s each ≈ 19s → payoff/outro hold ~5s.

## Music
Driving but premium instrumental bed at ~0.4. On-device cue unless a track is supplied. SFX whooshes (~0.2) on whips, one "snap" on the final logo lock.

## Outro / CTA
"Six brands. One launch." → PacificIQ leaf + wordmark lock → `pacificiq.com` · *The future of commerce.* Hold 4–5s.
