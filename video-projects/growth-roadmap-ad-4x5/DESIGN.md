# growth-roadmap-ad — Design Spec

Format: 4:5 Meta feed · 1080x1350 @ 30fps · Safe area ~10% margins.

Brand kit copied from `assets/ecomiq/`. Full reference: `assets/ecomiq/BRAND.md`.
Tokens live in `assets/brand-tokens.css`; fonts in `assets/fonts/fonts.css` (local).

## This project's idea
- Hook: Shopify founders at $20K/mo aiming for $100K/mo (Sean to camera + roadmap card)
- Message: we find the growth ceiling, fix the main blocker first, track the result, and map the gap
- CTA: Free discovery call, BOOK NOW (Flame pill), choose a time

## Visual identity: GROWTH ROADMAP (distinct from the profit-leak and 3 Changes ads)
- Recurring motif: CURRENT (filled dot, #9CD4FF) to TARGET (hollow ring, #FF4C32) on a dashed Sky #DEEEFE path
- Over Sean: horizontal road in a navy chest card (y 1040 to 1290)
- Full-screen beats: vertical road on Navy #06284C with a 4.5% Sky grid, ceiling = Flame bar above the progress line
- Ceiling splits into BLOCKER cells, a crosshair locks the one to FIX FIRST, progress resumes past it, THE GAP bracket at the end
- Type: Rethink Sans 700/800, one Hedvig serif italic word per headline (#9CD4FF)
- Subtitles: Rethink 700 56px white, bottom edge fixed at y=1450, thin navy stroke, about 9% of words highlighted

## What NOT to do
- No green for positive metrics, no extra hues, no gradients
- No 01/02/03 cards, no timers, no leak or margin imagery (those belong to the other campaign ads)
- No Sweet E's, Dryft or Rebuy-stat footage near the Mob Armor claim
- No confetti, cash, rockets or exploding graphs
