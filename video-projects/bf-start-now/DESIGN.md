# bf-start-now — Design Spec

A **talking-head short** cut to a 24.9s segment of Nate's "Social Black Friday —
start now" recording. Face + synced motion graphics + karaoke captions, in the
EcomIQ system.

Formats: **9:16** 1080x1920 (master) · **4:5** 1080x1350. Both 30fps, 24.9s,
with the voiceover as the audio track.

The 9:16 project owns structure, copy and all timing — timing is locked to the
voiceover and must not drift. The 4:5 is generated by `scripts/build-variants.mjs`,
which swaps the face-crop constants and appends the layout deltas in
`scripts/overrides/4x5.css`. Never hand-edit the variant.

## Source and timing
- Source: `2c: Social Black Friday - start now.mov` — 49.1s, ProRes 4K, 25fps.
- The segment used is **20.40s → 45.33s** of that file, re-encoded to
  `assets/vo.mp4` (1920x1080, H.264, 30fps) so the face wrapper has a clean
  landscape source.
- Word timings come from `npx hyperframes transcribe --model small.en` and are
  embedded in `assets/words.js`. **Nothing is fetched at render time.**

### What is actually said
The transcript supplied with the brief was a rough auto-caption and differs from
the recording in several places ("your margin's actually going to support" vs the
spoken "your margin can actually support"; "rip all the profit out of your Black
Friday" is in the audio but not in the supplied text). **Captions follow the
audio, not the supplied text** — a caption that disagrees with the voice is worse
than no caption.

## Layout
| Band | 9:16 | 4:5 |
|---|---|---|
| Graphics zone | y 190–850 | y 96–496 |
| Caption band | y 880–1090 | y 500–670 |
| Seam (feathers the face's top edge) | y 1082 | y 566 |
| Face, BOTTOM mode | y 1110–1920 | y 594–1350 |

Face modes — the crop that fills 1080x1920 is not the crop that fills 1080x1350,
so each format has its own pair:

| | BOTTOM | FULLSCREEN |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | `x -180, y 1110, scale 0.75` | `x -1166.5, y 0, scale 1.7778` |
| 4:5 | `x -132, y 594, scale 0.70` | `x -660, y 0, scale 1.25` |

BOTTOM crops the studio dead-space either side of the speaker rather than using
the naive exact-fit scale, which leaves him marooned in a band of empty set.

## Scenes — every anchor is a spoken word
| # | Start | Dur | Beat | Lands on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00 | 3.84 | The numbers: $114.70 order, three costs off, $59.71 left | "numbers," 1.15 |
| 2 | 3.84 | 3.12 | Discount dial sweeping to a flame ceiling | "support." 6.44 |
| 3 | 6.96 | 4.16 | Two cards: stock forecast, ad-spend ceiling | "stock" 8.28, "advertising." 10.27 |
| 4 | 11.12 | 2.96 | **START NOW** over a fullscreen face, weeks-to-BF strip | "start" 12.17, "now," 12.48 |
| 5 | 14.08 | 4.16 | Ad spend eats the contribution bar | "rip" 14.87 → "Black Friday." 17.00 |

Face mode changes at 1.15 (→BOTTOM), 10.84 (→FULLSCREEN), 13.93 (→BOTTOM), each
started before the incoming scene's content so the move is never a snap.

## No invented figures
Every number on screen is the workbook's own worked example: **$114.70** order
value, **$34.00 / $4.50 / $8.60** costs, **$59.71** contribution. Scenes 2 and 3
deliberately show bars and dials against labelled ceilings with **no numeric
readout** — the real answer depends on the viewer's business, and a specific
percentage or spend figure on screen would read as a claim. In scene 5 the
$59.71 does not count down to an invented remainder; it is swallowed by the
advancing spend bar and the stamp carries the point.

## What NOT to do
- Don't re-time anything without re-deriving from `assets/words.js`. The scene
  starts and the face-mode array are all in voiceover time.
- Don't put a caption line on screen while the previous one is still fading —
  clear out before the next line's first word minus 0.13s.
- Don't let the seam stop above the video's top edge; it has to overlap and
  feather it, or there is a razor line across the frame.
- Don't add a second hot accent. Flame orange only.
- Don't caption the supplied transcript instead of the audio.
