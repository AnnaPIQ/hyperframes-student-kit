# Profit Leak | Discovery call (9:16) — Design

Source of truth for the build: `EDITING-PLAN.md` in this folder. This file is the short brand summary the composition traces back to.

## Style Prompt
Calm, premium EcomIQ performance ad. Sean is one element inside a designed navy composition (cut-out, card, full frame) while a single profit-leak diagram evolves beside him. No hard cuts, no whips, no glitches. Every graphic holds long enough to read on a phone.

## Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background, panels, gradients | Navy `#06284C` |
| Revenue, money in, scan line, one subtitle highlight | Blue Tint `#9CD4FF` |
| Profit, main text, subtitles | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Costs, inactive leaks, dividers, flow lines | Sky Blue `#DEEEFE` |
| The leak, fix first, BOOK NOW | Flame Orange `#FF4C32` |

## Typography
- Rethink Sans (local woff2): 800 headlines, 700 labels, 600 eyebrows (+8% tracking) and subtitles.
- No serif in this ad. One italic subtitle phrase only ("we'll tell you").

## Format
1080 x 1920, 25 fps, 38.0 s. Graphic zone y 270 to 1120. Subtitle band y 1130 to 1250. Nothing important below y 1250 (Reels UI).

## What NOT to Do
- No green for positive numbers, no invented dollar results.
- Word highlights are rationed so they land: in subtitles, ONE colour highlight (`Book Now`, orange) and ONE italic (`we'll tell you`) for the whole ad. Never highlight a word that a graphic is already showing in colour at the same moment.
- Orange only marks the leak itself (LEAK 02, the leak segment and ring, the leak dot) and the BOOK NOW button. Headlines like TOO LITTLE and PROFIT LEAK stay white.
- Blue Tint as a word highlight only on `8- & 9-FIGURE`; otherwise it is data colour (revenue, $20K+).
- No text over Sean's cream vest without navy behind it.
- No flash cuts, no graphic on screen under 1.5 s.
- No third-party stats (e.g. sponsor slides) visible behind the 8- & 9-figure claim.
